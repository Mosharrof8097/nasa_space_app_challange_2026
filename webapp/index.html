EmberSync

One record, one truth, one step ahead of the flames.

Two satellites watch Earth burn, but they count fire differently. EmberSync merges their records into a single, trustworthy timeline.

Data scope

Key numbers

01

The broken timeline, repaired

Sharper VIIRS pixels split one physical fire into several detections. In raw FIRMS counts that looks like fire activity jumping overnight in 2012. It didn't. Nothing burned more.

FRP = Fire Radiative Power, the heat energy a fire gives off (megawatts). A white ring marks a fire confirmed by both satellites. Click any dot for details.

03

Burning Activity Calendar

One cell per day. Brighter means more fire events. A Z-score engine flags days that run well above a biome's baseline.

    04

    Per-biome harmonization

    Fire physics differs by terrain, so the genetic algorithm tunes each biome separately and is validated on fires both satellites saw on the same day.
